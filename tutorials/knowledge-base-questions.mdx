## Overview

Bland mines your call and SMS transcripts for questions your agents couldn't answer and collects them as knowledge gaps. Review them in the [Questions tab](https://app.bland.ai/dashboard/knowledge-base/questions) of the Knowledge Base dashboard, then answer each question to close the gap.

Each question shows how many times it was asked, where it came from, and its current status:

- **Open**: the question has no answer yet.
- **Resolved**: you answered the question and saved the answer to a knowledge base source.
- **Dismissed**: you marked the question as not worth answering.

Questions also appear on each agent's Questions tab, scoped to the knowledge base sources attached to that agent.

## View the source conversation

Every occurrence of a question links back to the conversation it was mined from. Next to each occurrence, click **View call** (voice) or **View message** (SMS) to open the Conversations page with that conversation's detail panel already open. Use it to read the surrounding transcript and understand what the caller was actually asking before you write an answer.

Questions asked more than once show a **+N more** button listing every past occurrence, each with its own **View call** or **View message** link.

On the Conversations page:

- The list narrows to that one conversation, and its transcript opens automatically.
- Closing the panel reveals a clearable **Conversation** filter chip. Clear it to return to your full conversation list.
- The link overrides any pinned agent or saved view filter, so the linked conversation is always visible.

Occurrences from some older SMS conversations have no conversation record to open, so they don't show a link.

## Manage questions

From each question's row you can:

- **Answer**: write an answer, pick the knowledge base source to save it to, and resolve the question.
- **Dismiss**: hide a question you don't want to answer. Dismissed questions can be reopened later.
- **Reopen**: move a resolved or dismissed question back to open.
- **Link to Existing**: merge a question into another existing question.
- **Delete**: remove the question permanently.

The filter bar lets you narrow by source and status, and sort by most asked, least asked, most recent, or oldest. **Export CSV** downloads every question matching your active filters.
