---
title: "Call logs"
description: "Find call records, transcripts, and recordings in Conversations."
---

Call logs now live in **Monitor → Conversations**. At **All agents**, the tabs are **Conversations**, **Active calls**, and **Contacts**. Inside an agent, use **Conversations** to see that agent's calls, messaging conversations, and web chats.

See [Conversations](/agents/conversations) for the current filters, call timeline, recordings, issue flags, and export steps. Use **Active calls** at All agents to read live transcripts or listen to calls when your organization enables listening.
