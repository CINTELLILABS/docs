## Overview

As an enterprise customer, your team gets a support portal inside the new Bland dashboard. You'll file tickets there, follow every reply, and see your organization's full ticket history in one place. There's no separate login and nothing to install.

Every ticket goes to Bland's support team, and the severity you set drives how quickly we respond.

<Info>
The portal is only in the new dashboard at [v2.app.bland.ai](https://v2.app.bland.ai), and it's turned on per organization. If you don't see a Tickets tab next to Contact support, ask your Bland account team to turn it on.
</Info>

## Find the portal

Click your organization's name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Contact support**. You'll see two tabs at the top of the page:

- Contact support, where you file a ticket.
- Tickets, where you'll find every ticket your team has filed, open and closed.

## Before your first ticket

A little setup helps the portal work well for your team:

1. Give the admin role to anyone who needs to see every ticket. Everyone else sees only the tickets they open.
2. Agree as a team on how you'll use severities. We recommend saving Critical for production outages.
3. Use the portal for anything that needs a tracked response, so the whole history stays in one place.

## File a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to the Contact support tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Give the ticket a one-line title and pick a severity. A topic is optional, but it helps: most topics ask a question or two, such as which pathway, which phone number, or which call IDs are affected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add detail and screenshots">
    Under "What's happening?", write what you expected, what happened instead, and anything that helps us reproduce it. You can attach up to five screenshots (PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP), each under 10 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the ticket">
    Click **Open ticket**. You'll land on your new ticket, and we'll reply there and by email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Severity levels

Pick the level that matches the impact on your business. It sets the priority on our side.

| Severity | Use it when |
|---|---|
| Critical | Production is down or calls are failing for everyone. |
| High | A core workflow is broken and there is no workaround. |
| Medium | Something is wrong but you can work around it. |
| Low | A question, a request, or a minor issue. |

### Topics

Pathways, Phone numbers / Telephony, Voice & latency, API / Integrations, Billing, Account, and Other. Every topic except Other asks one or two questions, so support has the context it needs on the first read.

## Track your tickets

The Tickets tab lists each ticket's title, severity, status, who opened it and when it was last updated.

- Switch between Open and Closed.
- Filter by severity, and sort by Last updated or Newest first.
- Your filters stay in the page URL, so you can bookmark a view or share it with a colleague in your organization.

Click a ticket to read the whole conversation, oldest first. The thread shows when a ticket was closed or reopened, and attachments appear as buttons you can click to download.

## Replies

Our replies show up on the ticket and also go by email to whoever opened it, at the email address on their Bland account. You can answer either way:

- Type in the box at the bottom of the ticket and click **Send reply**.
- Reply to the email. Your answer lands on the same ticket.

To reopen a closed ticket, reply from the portal. On a closed ticket, the button reads **Reply and reopen**.

## Who can see what

- Organization owners and admins see every ticket anyone in the organization has filed, and can filter the list by who opened it.
- Everyone else sees only the tickets they opened.

If you belong to more than one organization, you'll see the tickets for the organization you're working in.

## Limits

Each person can open up to 20 tickets and send up to 60 replies per hour. A title can be up to 200 characters, and a message up to 10,000.
