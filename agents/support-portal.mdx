## Overview

The Enterprise Support Portal is where your team opens support tickets, follows every reply, and sees your organization's ticket history. It's built into the Bland dashboard, so there's no separate login and nothing to install.

Every ticket goes to Bland's support team, and the severity you choose drives how quickly we respond.

<Info>
The portal is only in the new dashboard at [v2.app.bland.ai](https://v2.app.bland.ai), and it's turned on per organization. If you don't see a Tickets tab next to Contact support, ask your Bland account team to turn it on.
</Info>

## Before you begin

- Make sure you're signed in to the organization that has the problem. Tickets are filed under the organization you're working in, so switch organizations first if you belong to more than one.
- Check [status.bland.ai](https://status.bland.ai) for a known incident.

### Who can see tickets

Anyone in your organization can open a ticket. What they can see depends on their role:

| Role | Can see |
|---|---|
| Owner and admins | Every ticket in the organization, with a filter for who opened it |
| Everyone else | Only the tickets they opened |

Give the admin role to anyone on your team who needs to follow every ticket.

## Open a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Contact support">
    Click your organization's name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Contact support**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a title and severity">
    Write a one-line title that says what's wrong, then choose a severity. See [Choose a severity](#choose-a-severity).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a topic">
    A topic is optional, but it helps. Most topics ask a question or two, such as which pathway, which phone number, or which call IDs are affected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Under "What's happening?", describe the problem. See [What to include](#what-to-include) for the details that help us most.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach screenshots">
    Add up to five screenshots if they help. In the portal, you can attach files only when you open a ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the ticket">
    Click **Open ticket**. You'll land on your new ticket, and we'll reply there and by email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose a severity

Choose the level that matches the impact on your business right now.

| Severity | Use it when | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Production is down or calls are failing for everyone. | All outbound calls fail. Your inbound numbers stop answering. |
| High | An agent can't do a core part of its job, and there's no workaround. | Transfers fail on every call. Webhooks stop arriving. |
| Medium | Something is wrong but you can work around it. | One phone number fails, and you can route calls through another. |
| Low | A question, a request, or a minor issue. | How a feature works, a billing question, or a feature request. |

- Save Critical for production outages. We recommend agreeing as a team on how you'll use each level.
- You can't change the severity after you open a ticket. If the impact changes, reply on the ticket and tell us.

## What to include

A complete first message is the fastest way to a fix. Include:

- One issue per ticket. Open a separate ticket for each unrelated problem.
- What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
- When it started, with the date, time and time zone.
- Two or three example call IDs for a call problem.
- The agent, pathway or phone number involved.
- For an API problem, the endpoint, the request ID or timestamp, and the full error response.
- Steps to reproduce the problem.
- What you've already tried.
- How many calls or customers are affected.

<Warning>
Never put API keys, passwords or other secrets in a ticket.
</Warning>

## Track your tickets

The Tickets tab lists every ticket you can see, with its title, severity, status, who opened it and when it was last updated.

- Switch between Open and Closed.
- Filter by severity, and sort by Last updated or Newest first.
- Your filters stay in the page URL, so you can bookmark a view or share it with a colleague in your organization.

Click a ticket to read the whole conversation, oldest first. Attachments appear as buttons you can click to download.

### Ticket status

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Open | The ticket is active. We're working on it, or waiting on information from you. |
| Closed | Support closed the ticket. Reply on it to reopen it. |

Only support can close a ticket. The conversation shows each time a ticket was closed or reopened.

## Reply to a ticket

Our replies appear on the ticket and go by email to whoever opened it, at the email address on their Bland account. You can reply either way, and your answer lands on the same ticket:

- In the portal, type in the box at the bottom of the ticket and click **Send reply**.
- By email, reply to our message.

Keep follow-ups on the same ticket rather than opening a new one, so the whole history stays together.

To reopen a closed ticket, reply to it from the portal. On a closed ticket, the button reads **Reply and reopen**.

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Title | 200 characters |
| Message | 10,000 characters |
| Screenshots | 5 per ticket (PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP), 10 MB each |
| New tickets | 20 per person per hour |
| Replies | 60 per person per hour |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I close a ticket myself?">
    No. Support closes tickets. If your issue is resolved, reply on the ticket to let us know.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add a colleague to a ticket?">
    The portal doesn't have CCs. Owners and admins already see every ticket in the organization, so give the admin role to anyone who needs to follow along.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
