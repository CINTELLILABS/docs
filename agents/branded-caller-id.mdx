---
title: "Branded caller ID"
description: "Apply to show your business name, logo, and call reason on supported phone calls."
---

Branded caller ID requires Enterprise and carrier approval. Inside an agent, open **Build → Identity** and the branded caller ID application. Approval and the recipient's carrier and device determine what appears; a preview is not proof that every call will display the brand.

## Prepare business details

Use verified details that match your legal business: legal entity name, company type, industry, tax ID, business address and country, support phone and email, website, and an authorized representative's first name, last name, email, and title. Mismatches can delay or prevent approval.

## Complete the application

1. Complete **Business identity**.
2. Add **Brand assets**: a display name of at most 30 characters, a call reason of at most 60 characters, and a square PNG or JPEG logo of at most 2 MB. Review the preview and call category.
3. Select the **Phone numbers** to register. Review each number rather than assuming approval applies to all the agent's numbers.
4. Select **Carriers**. The current Bland application offers T-Mobile and Verizon. Confirm any other carrier's availability with your account team; provider announcements alone do not establish support in Bland's application.
5. Open **Review & submit**, resolve missing information, and submit. Monitor review status before using the branding in production.

Each carrier reviews independently. Review timelines vary; the dashboard's estimates are not approval guarantees. Rejections need correction and resubmission before branding can appear.

## Replace an application

An in-review or approved application is read-only. **Start a new application** replaces it and restarts carrier review. Branded calls stop until the replacement is approved. Review this consequence before confirming; editing a draft and replacing an approved application are different actions.
