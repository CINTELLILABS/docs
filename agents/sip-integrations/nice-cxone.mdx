Route calls from your NICE CXone contact center to a Bland agent. NICE and Bland already run a partner SIP trunk between CXone and Bland. To use it, you connect your CXone **Business Unit (BU)** to that trunk, then point numbers in your CXone routing at Bland.

<Info>
**NICE DEVone partner.** Bland is a NICE DEVone technology partner. NICE built and tested the Bland trunk once, over TLS with SRTP, and adds each customer Business Unit to it. A new customer does not need a new trunk or a repeat of the end-to-end trunk testing.
</Info>

## What you'll need

- A NICE CXone account, with your **Business Unit number** and your **NICE Account Manager**.
- Access to CXone Studio (or a NICE technical account manager who has it) to edit your routing script.
- A Bland account with SIP trunking enabled. If you don't see a **SIP** tab under **Phone numbers**, contact Bland support to turn it on.
- A Bland agent ready to answer the calls.

## Supported calling features

| Feature | Supported |
|---|---|
| Calls from CXone to your Bland agent | Yes |
| US and EU connections | Yes (`us2.sip.bland.ai` and `eu2.sip.bland.ai`) |
| Returning the caller to CXone | Arranged per deployment. See [Return calls to CXone](#return-calls-to-cxone) |

## How the connection works

```text
Caller ──> CXone (your BU) ──> NICE DEVone trunk ──SIP/TLS──> Bland agent
```

- The DEVone trunk is **multi-tenant**. Bland's partner connection is the parent, and your Business Unit is added to it as a child.
- The trunk carries calls **from CXone to Bland**. NICE assigns your Business Unit a block of routing numbers, and calls that your Studio script sends to those numbers arrive at Bland.
- Signaling uses TLS 1.2 on port 5061 and media uses SRTP. Bland accepts TLS only.
- **Billing for the SIP sessions stays between you and NICE.** NICE bills your Business Unit for the number of SIP sessions configured for it.

## Connect your Business Unit

### 1. Order the SIP sessions from NICE

Contact your **NICE Account Manager** and ask for a contract or addendum that adds SIP sessions (ports) for the **Bland DEVone partner connection** to your Business Unit. Include:

- Your Business Unit number
- The number of concurrent calls you want to send to Bland
- The region: US, EU, or both

This is usually the longest step. Start it first.

### 2. NICE connects your Business Unit to the Bland trunk

Once the addendum is signed, the NICE Connectivity team:

1. Adds your Business Unit to Bland's partner trunk with the number of sessions you ordered.
2. Assigns your Business Unit a block of routing numbers for Bland. These are usually virtual numbers in the `999` range (for example `+1999xxxxxxx`), not public phone numbers.

Ask your NICE contact to send the number range to you and to your Bland account team. Bland's point of contact at NICE can help if the request stalls.

### 3. Configure Bland

1. Send your Bland account team the routing numbers from NICE and the Bland organization they belong to. Bland attaches them to your organization on the NICE connection.
2. Assign an agent to each number. At **All agents**, go to **Dispatch**, then **Phone numbers**, find the number, click **Configure**, and under **Assignment** select the agent.

### 4. Route calls in CXone

In CXone Studio, update the script for the point of contact that should reach Bland, so it sends the call to one of your Bland routing numbers. NICE does not provide a template for this. Your CXone administrator or NICE technical account manager edits the script in your Business Unit.

### 5. Test

From CXone MAX, dial a Bland routing number as a test user that has the right skill, or call a point of contact linked to the updated script. The Bland agent should answer. NICE estimates one to two weeks from a signed addendum to working calls.

Open the call's [SIP trace](/agents/sip#inspect-a-call-sip-trace) on the trunk's **Overview** tab in Bland to see exactly what arrived.

## Return calls to CXone

The DEVone trunk only carries calls from CXone to Bland. If the caller needs to come back to a CXone agent after the Bland agent finishes, the return path is set up for your deployment, for example through the CXone APIs and your Studio script. Talk to your Bland account team before you build the script so both sides agree on the hand-back.

## Connection details

These are already configured on the Bland partner trunk. They are listed so your network and security teams can review them.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Bland SIP endpoints | `us2.sip.bland.ai` (US), `eu2.sip.bland.ai` (EU) |
| Transport and port | TLS 1.2 or higher, TCP port `5061` |
| Media | SRTP, UDP `16384` to `32768` |
| Bland certificate | Let's Encrypt. The ISRG Root X1 CA must be trusted |
| Number format | E.164 |

Bland's signaling and media IPs for each region are listed under [Connection Endpoints](/agents/sip#connection-endpoints).

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| "No Routes Found" or a bad-number error in MAX | The call never left your Business Unit | Ask NICE to confirm routing for your BU to the Bland trunk is set up on the cluster your BU runs on |
| Calls time out with `504` | NICE is sending to port 5060 or without TLS | Bland accepts TLS on port `5061` only. Ask NICE to check the trunk profile is still TLS |
| Call rejected with `603 Decline` | The routing number is not attached to your Bland organization | Send the number range to your Bland account team |
| Call rejected with `407` | The number is pointed at a Bland hostname other than the production endpoints | Ask NICE to route to `us2.sip.bland.ai` or `eu2.sip.bland.ai` |
| Call connects but there's no audio | Media blocked or not encrypted | Confirm SRTP is on and Bland's media IPs are allowed |
| The order seems stuck | The addendum is not yet signed | Follow up with your NICE Account Manager. NICE cannot provision sessions until the addendum is in place |
