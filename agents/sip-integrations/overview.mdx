These guides walk through connecting a specific carrier or contact center platform to Bland. Each one covers what to request from the provider, what to configure on each side, and the failures you are most likely to hit.

Every integration uses the same Bland SIP stack described in [SIP](/agents/sip): TLS signaling on port 5061, SRTP media, and regional endpoints with static IPs. The guides only add the provider-specific steps.

## Choosing a setup

| You have | Start with |
|---|---|
| Numbers on Bandwidth | [Bandwidth](/agents/sip-integrations/bandwidth) |
| A Five9 contact center | [Five9](/agents/sip-integrations/five9) |
| A NICE CXone contact center | [NICE CXone](/agents/sip-integrations/nice-cxone) |
| Any other carrier, PBX, or SBC | The [SIP Setup Wizard](/agents/sip#sip-setup-wizard) |
| Numbers you want to move to Bland | [Number porting](/agents/sip#number-porting) |

<Note>
Don't see your provider? Any SIP endpoint that supports TLS signaling and SRTP media can connect through the [SIP Setup Wizard](/agents/sip#sip-setup-wizard). For an enterprise rollout with a provider not listed here, contact your Bland account team.
</Note>
