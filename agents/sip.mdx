SIP connects your carrier or PBX to Bland for inbound and outbound phone calls. It requires the **SIP** entitlement; ask support to enable it for your organization.

At **All agents**, open **Dispatch → Phone numbers → SIP**. SIP trunks are organization resources. Inside one agent, switch to All agents to manage them.

## Prerequisites

- A reachable SIP endpoint on your carrier or PBX.
- Access to the firewall and authentication settings.
- The phone numbers or DIDs you intend to route.

## Create and configure a trunk

Select **Create trunk**. The wizard has **Provider**, **Discovery**, **Firewall**, **Authentication**, **Numbers**, and **Complete** steps. Enter your **SIP endpoint**, check the TLS connection, allow the regional IP addresses and ports, and configure the authentication your provider requires. Numbers is optional; direction is chosen per number.

**Save trunk** in Authentication creates the trunk before numbers are attached. Leaving after that step can leave an empty trunk to finish configuring later.

Open the trunk card for **Overview**, **Numbers**, and **Configure**. **Test connection** sends SIP OPTIONS over TLS; it does not place a phone call. Calls appear in call records with a manual refresh, not a live trace stream.

## Route calls

Attach E.164 phone numbers, DIDs, or extensions on **Numbers**. Configure the number's **Inbound** tab to choose the agent and inbound settings. For outbound calls, use a trunk-bound number as `from` in [Send a call](/api-v1/post/calls).

Bland accepts TLS signaling on port 5061 and no plaintext SIP listener on port 5060. Fetch current regional signaling and media rules from [Firewall IPs](/api-v1/get/sip-firewall-ips) before configuring your firewall.

## Detailed setup and recovery

The [SIP integration guide](/enterprise-features/SIP-integration) covers endpoint formats, discovery, authentication, number porting, firewall rules, connection troubleshooting, and the API. Use that reference for current settings and limits.

Porting requires carrier-matching account details, authorization, and proof of ownership. Confirm carrier lead times and the accepted port date with support before moving service.
