## Overview

The Enterprise Support Portal lives inside your Bland dashboard. Your team files tickets there, follows every reply, and sees the full history of your organization's requests in one place. There is no separate login and nothing to install.

Every ticket is handled by Bland's support engineers under your enterprise support agreement. The severity you set drives how quickly we respond.

<Info>
The portal is enabled per organization. If you do not see a Tickets tab under Support, ask your Bland account team to turn it on.
</Info>

## Where to find it

In the dashboard, open Support. You will see two tabs:

- Contact support: the ticket form.
- Tickets: every ticket your team has filed, open and closed.

## File a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to Support and stay on the Contact support tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Give the ticket a short title, then pick a severity and a topic. The form asks a few questions that depend on the topic, such as which pathway, which phone number, or which call IDs are affected. Answering them is the fastest way to a first response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add detail and screenshots">
    Write what happened, what you expected, and when it started. Attach up to five screenshots (PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP, 10 MB each).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit**. The ticket opens right away and appears in the Tickets tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Severity levels

Pick the level that matches the impact on your business. It sets the priority on our side.

| Severity | Use it when |
|---|---|
| Critical | Production is down or calls are failing for everyone. |
| High | A core workflow is broken and there is no workaround. |
| Medium | Something is wrong but you can work around it. |
| Low | A question, a request, or a minor issue. |

### Topics

Pathways, Phone numbers / Telephony, Voice & latency, API / Integrations, Billing, Account, and Other. Each topic adds one or two questions so that support has the context it needs on the first read.

## Track your tickets

The Tickets tab lists your organization's tickets with their severity, topic, who opened them and when they were last updated.

- Switch between Open and Closed.
- Filter by severity, and sort by last updated or newest first.
- Filters are kept in the page URL, so you can bookmark a view or share it with a colleague in your organization.

Open a ticket to read the whole conversation, oldest first. Attachments appear as downloadable buttons.

## Replies

Bland's replies show up on the ticket in the portal and are also sent to the email address on your Bland account. You can answer either way:

- Reply from the ticket page in the portal.
- Reply to the email. It lands on the same ticket.

Replying to a closed ticket reopens it automatically.

## Who can see what

- Organization owners and admins see every ticket filed by anyone in the organization, and can filter by who opened it.
- Other members see only the tickets they opened themselves.

Access follows your Bland login, so tickets are always tied to the organization you are working in.

## Limits

To keep the queue healthy, each user can file up to 20 tickets and send up to 60 replies per hour.

## Getting started with the portal

When your organization is onboarded, your account team will confirm the portal is on. Before your first ticket:

1. Check that the people who need to see all tickets have the admin role in your organization.
2. Agree internally on how your team uses severities. Reserve Critical for production outages.
3. Keep Slack or email for coordination, and use the portal for anything that needs a tracked response.
