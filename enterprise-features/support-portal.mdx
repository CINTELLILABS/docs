The support portal lets enterprise organizations open support tickets from the **Support** page in the dashboard. You can follow every ticket your organization has opened, and reply without leaving Bland. Bland support answers on the ticket and by email.

## Prerequisites

- An enterprise organization with the support portal turned on. To turn it on, contact your Bland account team.

If the portal isn't on for your organization, the **Support** page shows the standard contact form instead.

## Who sees which tickets

- Organization owners and admins see every ticket in the organization.
- Other members see only the tickets they opened.

## Open a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Support page">
    Go to **Support** in the dashboard sidebar. The **Contact support** tab opens the ticket form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the issue">
    Fill in the form:

    - **Title**: one line that says what's wrong.
    - **Severity**: **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, or **Urgent**.
    - **Topic (optional)**: what the ticket is about. Some topics ask for extra details, such as a pathway, a phone number, or call IDs.
    - **What's happening?**: what you expected, what happened instead, and anything that helps reproduce it.
    - **Screenshots (optional)**: up to 5 images, each under 10MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Submit the form. The ticket appears under the **Tickets** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track tickets

The **Tickets** tab lists your organization's tickets with their title, severity, status, and who opened them. You can narrow the list:

- **Open** or **Closed**: switch between active and resolved tickets.
- **Severity**: show one severity, or **Any severity**.
- **Opened by**: show tickets from a specific teammate or **Opened by me**. This filter is available to owners and admins.
- **Sort**: **Last updated** or **Newest first**.

Select a ticket to see its full conversation, including attachments.

## Reply to a ticket

Open a ticket and add your message in the **Reply** box at the bottom of the conversation. Use it to add detail or answer a question from Bland support.

Replying to a closed ticket reopens it. On a closed ticket, the box is labeled **Reply and reopen**.

Replies from Bland support appear on the ticket and in your email.
