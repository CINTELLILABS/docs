You can use the Bland dashboard with a keyboard and a screen reader to build and publish voice agents, review conversations and recordings, manage teammates and contact support.

## Tested browsers and assistive technology

| Assistive technology or input | Browser | Operating system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| JAWS 2027 | Chrome 158 | Windows Server 2025 |
| NVDA 2026.3.1 | Chrome 158 | Windows Server 2025 |
| VoiceOver | Chrome 158 | macOS 15.8.2 |
| Keyboard only, including 200% browser zoom | Chrome 158 | Windows Server 2025 |

Testing covers desktop Chrome. Other browsers, Windows 10 and 11, mobile devices, screen magnifiers, speech input, switch access and forced colors modes are outside this coverage.

## Use the dashboard with a keyboard

- When you open a signed-in page, press Tab to reach **Skip to main content**, then press Enter to move past the navigation.
- Press Ctrl+K on Windows or Cmd+K on macOS to open **Search pages and settings**. Type a page name and press Enter to open it. Search includes **Contact support** and **Tickets**.
- Press Escape to close a dialog. In a code editor, Tab inserts an indent. To leave the editor, press Escape, then Tab. With NVDA in focus mode, press Escape twice, then Tab.
- On the agent builder canvas, press Enter or Space on a step to open it. You can also use **Search nodes** to find a step by name.
- To reorder an item such as a variable, focus its **Drag to reorder** handle. Press Space to pick it up, use the arrow keys to move it, then press Space to drop it. Press Escape to cancel.
- In **Conversations** or **Tickets**, focus a row and press Enter to open it. To resize the conversation details panel, focus the **Resize panel** handle and press the Left Arrow or Right Arrow key.

## Use the dashboard with a screen reader

Use the navigation, banner and main landmarks to move around signed-in pages. Page titles identify where you are. For example, the **Agents** page has the title **Agents · Bland**.

If you verify by SMS during sign-up or sign-in, enter all 6 digits within 5 minutes. The form checks the code when you enter the sixth digit. Your screen reader reads these instructions when you reach the code field. If the SMS code expires, choose **Send a new code**.

On **Contact support**, an error message identifies the field that needs attention and moves focus to it.

## Zoom, contrast and call recordings

- You can zoom the dashboard to 200%. On **Environments**, expand the **Production** row to read the full version and deployment time if the summary is cut off.
- To strengthen field outlines and dividers, open **Settings**, select **Account**, and turn on **Increase contrast** under **Appearance**.
- Status badges include text labels, field errors have a message next to the field, and links in running text are underlined. Text in the tested workflows meets the WCAG 2.0 Level AA contrast threshold.
- Call recordings play when you choose **Play recording**. You can pause playback or use **Skip back 10 seconds** and **Skip forward 10 seconds**. The **Transcript** tab has the text of the call with speaker labels and timestamps.

## Known limitations

- JAWS skips message text on the **Transcript** tab and the notice on calls without a recording. NVDA and VoiceOver read both.
- At 400% browser zoom, some controls, including **Publish agent**, can be out of reach. Some pages also require scrolling in two directions. Use a wider browser window or reduce zoom to 200% to reach those controls.
- The focus outline on text fields can be hard to see.
- Screen readers may pronounce customer-written names in another language as English.

## Get accessibility help

You can open and resolve a support case entirely by email at [support@bland.ai](mailto:support@bland.ai). The support team also answers questions live during office hours in the [Bland Discord community](https://discord.gg/QvxDz8zcKe).

To report an accessibility problem, email us or open **Contact support** in the dashboard. Include the page, what you were trying to do, what happened, and the browser and assistive technology you used.

You can also email Support for Bland's Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR) or the supporting test evidence. The report covers WCAG 2.0 Levels A and AA and the Revised Section 508 standards. If you require an independent accessibility audit, let us know.
